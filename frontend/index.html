⚠️ The Problem

Mental Health Crises Go Unnoticed

People express distress indirectly on social media — hopelessness, isolation, goodbye messages — but no one is listening at scale.

😶

Indirect Expression

Distress is rarely direct. People post hopelessness, isolation, and farewell language that goes unrecognized by friends and algorithms alike.

⏰

Too Late to Help

Friends and family realize crisis moments only after the fact. Early signals are missed because there's no system to track emotional trends.

🏥

No Scalable System

🔄 Workflow

How PulseNet Works

1

You Post on Social Media

PulseNet's browser extension runs in the background with your consent, observing your own posts only.

2

Extension Captures Captions

Post text is extracted privately and sent to the PulseNet backend API for analysis.

3

AI Scores the Text

NLP model analyzes sentiment, crisis keywords, and isolation language to produce a Distress Score (0–100).

4

Dashboard Updates

Your 7-day emotional trend chart updates in real time. You see patterns you might not notice yourself.

5

Guardian Notified

If score crosses your set threshold, a trusted guardian receives an automated alert with helpline resources.
